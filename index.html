Meet the photographer

Hello, I’m Himin

Finding beauty in the honest details.

I’m a self-taught photographer based in Kerala, India. I’m inspired by people, gentle light, rich earthy colours and the emotion that can live inside a simple frame.

Whether we’re creating a model portfolio, a fashion story or a personal portrait, I want the photographs to feel considered without ever feeling forced.

Texture, fabric, movement and thoughtful styling can give a portrait its own quiet character.

Follow the natural light.

Soft shadows and directional light help shape a photograph while keeping the feeling natural.
