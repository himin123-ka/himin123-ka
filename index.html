About

Portraits, stories,
emotions

I’m Himin, a solo photographer from Palakkad. I photograph people, little ones and families across Palakkad, Malappuram and Thrissur. My work lives in quiet moments: a glance, a half-smile, a held breath.

Sessions are relaxed and unhurried. I guide gently, shoot in soft natural light and edit every photograph by hand, so you recognise yourself, and the feeling of the moment, in every frame.

Questions

Good to know

When should I book?

Message me with your preferred date and location and I’ll confirm availability.

Which areas do you cover?

I’m based in Palakkad and photograph across Palakkad, Malappuram and Thrissur. I’m happy to travel further; share your location when you enquire.

Do you photograph babies and kids?

Yes. Little Ones sessions are relaxed and led by the child, with parents welcome in the frame.

What should I wear?

Soft, solid colours or a favourite traditional outfit work beautifully. We can plan the look together before the session.

How do I receive my photographs?

Through a private online gallery in full quality, ready to download and share.
