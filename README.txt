HIMIN MOHAN PHOTOGRAPHY — REFERENCE-INSPIRED PORTFOLIO

1. Upload index.html to the ROOT of your GitHub repository.
2. In GitHub, open Settings > Pages, choose Deploy from a branch, branch main, folder /(root), then Save.
3. Wait a few minutes and open your GitHub Pages URL.

IMPORTANT CUSTOMIZATION:
- Replace the Unsplash sample photo URLs in index.html with your own image URLs/photos.
- Replace https://www.instagram.com/ with your real Instagram profile URL.
- Replace hello@example.com with your real contact email.
